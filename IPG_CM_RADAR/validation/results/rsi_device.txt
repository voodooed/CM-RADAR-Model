==============================================================================
   range axis  : 0 .. 100.0 m over 128 bins -> 0.7874 m/bin
   doppler axis: (-30.0, 30.0) m/s over 64 bins -> 0.9524 (m/s)/bin

   a) a target that lands exactly on a bin keeps its amplitude
      (documented normalisation: 'All detections are normalized to compensate
       for any signal processing gain or loss')
         r [m]   v [m/s]  peak bin r  peak bin v   |A| peak   |A| in
         25.20     10.00          32          42     1.0000   1.0000  ok 
         50.39    -12.86          64          18     0.5000   0.5000  ok 
         75.59     -0.48          96          31     2.0000   2.0000  ok 
      amplitude normalisation preserved (documented): ok 

   b) resolution: two equal targets merge below ~1 range bin
      separation 0.25 bins (0.197 m) -> 1 peak(s)
      separation 0.50 bins (0.394 m) -> 1 peak(s)
      separation 1.00 bins (0.787 m) -> 1 peak(s)
      separation 2.00 bins (1.575 m) -> 2 peak(s)

   c) window functions and spectral leakage
      target placed half a bin off-grid (worst case for leakage)
      Rectangular : peak 0.6366, scalloping loss  -3.92 dB, leakage 3+ bins away  -16.89 dBc
      Hann        : peak 0.8510, scalloping loss  -1.40 dB, leakage 3+ bins away  -40.26 dBc
      (Hann trades a larger scalloping loss for much lower leakage - the
       documented reason for offering the two windows)

   d) aliasing: a Doppler beyond the unambiguous interval folds back
      v =   +12.86 m/s -> doppler bin  45 (unambiguous interval = 60.0 m/s)  ok 
      v =   +72.86 m/s -> doppler bin  45 (unambiguous interval = 60.0 m/s)  ok

      folding into the unambiguous interval: ok

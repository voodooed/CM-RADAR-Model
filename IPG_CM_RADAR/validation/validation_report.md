# Validation report

Run against a **CarMaker 15.1** installation (`carmaker/win64-15.1`).
Reproduce with `./validation/run_all.sh <install-dir>` (or set `CARMAKER_DIR`);
raw outputs are in `validation/results/`.

---

## 0. What validation is and is not possible

The radar models themselves ship only as compiled code
(`lib/libcarmaker.a`, `GUI/IPGRsi.dll`). The IPG EULA §4(ii) prohibits
decompiling, disassembling or reverse engineering, and **no disassembly was
performed for this work**. CarMaker is also a Windows build and was not executed
here, so there are **no reference simulation logs to compare against**.

That rules out the strongest form of validation (same scenario in, same numbers
out). What remains, and what was done:

| Level | Possible? | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Bit-for-bit against a CarMaker run | no (no reference run, Windows-only binary) | — |
| Against **IPG's own shipped data files** | **yes** | §1, §2 |
| Against the **documented equations** | **yes** | §3 |
| Against **closed-form physics** the manual says the model implements | **yes** | §5 |
| Against **documented qualitative behaviour** | **yes** | §4, §6 |
| Undocumented internals (road clutter σ₀, damping coefficients, mirror-target probability law) | no | inferred, flagged everywhere |

Nothing below should be read as "our implementation equals IPG's". It says:
*where IPG published something checkable, we reproduce it; elsewhere we state
what we assumed.*

## 2. Antenna model vs. IPG's own generated map  →  `results/antenna_map.txt`

**This is the strongest check in the package.** `Data/Sensor/Radar_Default` was
generated by IPG's Antenna Gain Map Generator from `BeamWidth = 20 15`,
`ScanRange = 0 0`, `AntennaEff = 1.0`. Our independent implementation of
Reference Manual Equations 507/508 must reproduce that 181 × 181 matrix.

Structural agreement (these fix the aperture dimensions `a`, `b`):

| | file | Eq. 507/508 theory |
|---|---|---|
| first azimuth null | 23.0° | `asin(λ/a) = 23.21°` |
| first elevation null | 17.0° | `asin(λ/b) = 17.19°` |
| 3 dB azimuth width | 18.0° (1° grid) | parameter 20° |
| 3 dB elevation width | 14.0° (1° grid) | parameter 15° |
| peak gain | 20.3655 dB | `4π a b/λ² = 20.3310 dB` (Δ = −0.0345 dB) |

Shape agreement, normalised to the peak:

| region | Eq. 507/508 only | + inferred obliquity `(1+cos Θ)/2` |
|---|---|---|
| inside the 3 dB main lobe | mean 0.038 dB, max 0.088 dB | **mean 0.012 dB, max 0.028 dB** |
| inside the 10 dB main lobe | mean 0.119 dB, max 0.300 dB | **mean 0.042 dB, max 0.119 dB** |
| everywhere above −25 dB | mean 1.318 dB, max 6.114 dB | **mean 0.120 dB, max 0.597 dB** |
| everywhere above −40 dB | mean 3.128 dB, max 6.259 dB | **mean 0.210 dB, max 2.801 dB** |

Conclusions:

* the documented Equations 507/508, interpreted with the direction cosines
  `u_y = cos(el)·sin(az)`, `u_z = sin(el)`, are **confirmed** — null positions
  match to 0.2° and the main lobe to 0.09 dB;
* the residual at large off-boresight angles is a function of the polar angle
  only (verified: azimuth-cut and elevation-cut residuals coincide at equal Θ),
  i.e. an **obliquity factor**. `(1 + cos Θ)/2` (the Huygens/Kirchhoff factor)
  brings the whole map to 0.12 dB mean. This factor is **not documented**; it is
  our inference and is switchable (`AntennaGainMap.generate(..., obliquity=)`);
* the 0.035 dB peak offset suggests IPG normalises the directivity numerically
  from the sampled pattern rather than using `4π a b/λ²` exactly. Not resolved.

For production use, load `Radar_Default` directly (supported) and the question
disappears.

## 5. Model 2 propagation vs. closed-form physics  →  `results/rsi_propagation.txt`

The manual states the scattered fields are "an analytical solution to the
Maxwell's equation and are, other than the restriction to the far-field, an
exact solution of physical laws". The reference implementation must therefore
reproduce the analytic answers.

**Flat perfectly conducting plate, normal incidence** — physical optics says
`σ = 4πA²/λ²`. Plates kept in the far field (`R > 2D²/λ`), 120 000 rays:

| plate | R | σ theory | σ simulated | error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.08 m | 30 m | 15.31 dBm² | 15.64 | +0.33 dB |
| 0.08 m | 60 m | 15.31 | 13.58 | −1.73 dB |
| 0.10 m | 30 m | 19.19 | 19.12 | −0.06 dB |
| 0.10 m | 60 m | 19.19 | 20.62 | +1.43 dB |
| 0.15 m | 30 m | 26.23 | 26.62 | +0.39 dB |
| 0.15 m | 60 m | 26.23 | 25.60 | −0.63 dB |

**mean |error| 0.76 dB, max 1.73 dB** — the residual is ray-count quantisation
(the number of rays that land on a small plate is a small integer).

**Range law**: −13.08, −12.07, −12.11 dB per doubling of range (theory −12.04).

**Doppler**: plate at −8 m/s, 484 interaction points → mean radial velocity
−8.0000 m/s, mean range 30.0000 m (exact).

**Road multipath**: sensor at 0.5 m, target at 0.75 m over asphalt, direct path
vs. direct + road:

| R | interference |
|---|---|
| 20 m | −2.06 dB |
| 25 m | +2.10 dB |
| 30 m | −3.85 dB |
| 35 m | −1.31 dB |
| 40 m | +0.96 dB |

i.e. constructive/destructive fringes oscillating with range — exactly the
documented effect ("a phase shift can result in interference effects, which lead
to signal strength increasing or decreasing at the sensor").

This validates the *kernel*, not IPG's kernel. IPG's exact PO formulation,
roughness statistics and bounce budget are unknown.

## 7. End-to-end examples

**Model 1** (`results/example_object_list.txt`) — ACC-like scene with the real
`Radar_Default` antenna and the real `RCS_Car` / `RCS_Truck` / `RCS_Pedestrian`
maps: lead car at 60 m and truck at 90 m detected with SNR 47–58 dB and
`ProbDetect = 1.000`; the pedestrian at 40 m sits near threshold
(SNR ≈ 14 dB, `ProbDetect` 0.53…0.63) and drops in and out — the correct
qualitative behaviour for a pedestrian at that range.

**Model 2** (`results/example_rsi.txt`) — 4.5 m metal car body over an asphalt
road with a guardrail, using the real `RadarRSI_Default` transceiver and
`MaterialLib`:

| truth | reported |
|---|---|
| front face at 38.35 m, −5 m/s, 0° | 38.45 m, −4.89 m/s, −0.12° |
| front face at 23.35 m, −5 m/s, 0° | 23.45 m, −4.90 m/s, −0.11° |

The detection lands on the **nearest face**, not the bounding-box centre — the
expected behaviour of a ray-traced radar and the main qualitative difference to
Model 1, which always reports the bounding-box centre.

## 8. Known discrepancies and open questions

1. **Antenna peak gain** differs by 0.035 dB from `Radar_Default`; the obliquity
   factor is inferred (§2).
2. **Road clutter** (Model 1): functional form and σ₀ are inferred. This is the
   largest single uncertainty in Model 1's detection ranges for small targets.
3. **Atmospheric / rain / fog damping**: IPG cites DESK, ITU-R P.838-3 and
   Brooker but publishes no coefficients. Ours are public literature values;
   absolute damping will differ.
4. **Extended-object RCS averaging** (Model 1) and the **RCS noise correction**
   are interpretations of one-sentence descriptions.
5. **Mirror false positives** (Model 1): the probability law is invented; only
   its documented dependencies (truck vs. car, sensor–object distance,
   object–wall distance) and the ID convention are reproduced.
6. **PO scattering kernel** (Model 2) is our choice; it is validated against the
   plate RCS but IPG's formulation is unknown.
7. **RD-map synthesis** (Model 2) uses the analytic DTFT kernel instead of
   generating and transforming beat-signal samples. Mathematically equivalent for
   an ideal FMCW chain; it does not reproduce ADC quantisation or non-idealities
   that IPG may model.
8. **Elevation angular processing** (Model 2) is not implemented; it needs a 2-D
   virtual array and is off in the shipped example parameterisation.
9. **Random streams** differ from IPG's even with the same seed; only the
   distributions match.
10. Equation 509 (missing minus sign) and Equation 524 (`theta` where `phi`
    belongs) appear to be **typos in the CarMaker 15.1 Reference Manual**; we
    implement the physically consistent versions and flag both.
